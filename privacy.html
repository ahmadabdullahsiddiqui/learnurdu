Privacy Policy

Ustad — The Urdu Teacher · last updated 16 September 2026

Short version: Ustad collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no sign‑in, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app does not send your data anywhere.

What data the app processes

  • Learning state only, stored on your own device. Your progress, streak, spaced‑repetition schedule, quiz scores, chosen language and playback settings are saved in your browser's localStorage (under the key roshni.v1). This never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.
  • No personal data (no name, email, location, contacts, identifiers) is requested, collected, or stored.

Network activity

  • The app makes no application network requests at runtime. Fonts, pronunciation audio and all content are bundled and served from the app's own origin.
  • As with any website, the static host (GitHub Pages) receives the ordinary HTTP request needed to deliver the page, which may include your IP address and browser type in the host's standard server logs. This is processing by the host, not by Ustad, and is not used by the app. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Cookies & tracking

None. Ustad sets no cookies and uses no analytics, fingerprinting, or third‑party scripts.

Children

Ustad is designed to be safe for children. Because it collects no personal data and has no social, chat, purchase, or external‑link features that expose a child, it does not knowingly gather information from anyone, including children under 13/16.

Your control

All stored data is local to your browser. You can erase everything at any time by clearing the site's data / localStorage in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app/extension.

Browser extension

The Ustad extension requests no permissions (no access to tabs, browsing history, cookies, or other sites). It only opens the same offline app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above will change.
